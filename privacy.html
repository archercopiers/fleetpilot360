Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

FleetPilot360 is a brand of Archer Business Systems, Inc. This policy explains how we handle personal information connected with our marketing website, requests for information, and advertising inquiries.

1. Information we receive

We receive information you choose to provide when you email, call, request pricing, or submit a lead form through an advertising platform. This may include your name, company, email address, phone number, fleet size, services of interest, and the contents of your message.

Our website hosting and other technical service providers may process basic connection information, such as IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, and access times, to deliver and secure the website.

Please do not include passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers, or sensitive driver or employee information in a pricing inquiry.

2. Website contact form

The website pricing form opens your email application with your inquiry filled in. Completing the form alone does not send the inquiry to us. You must send the message through your email application. Your email provider handles that message under its own privacy practices, and we receive it when it is delivered to our business email.

3. How we use information

  • Respond to questions and provide pricing, product information, and requested demonstrations.
  • Follow up about your inquiry and help evaluate solutions for your business.
  • Maintain business correspondence and support customer relationships.
  • Operate, troubleshoot, and protect our website and communications.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and protect legal rights.

4. Advertising inquiries and Google

If you submit a Google Ads lead form, Google collects the information entered in that form and makes the lead available to us. We use those details to respond to your request and follow up about FleetPilot360 services. Google's handling of information is described in its Privacy Policy.

Advertising platforms may independently process information about your interactions with their ads, including clicks, device information, and identifiers, under their own policies. You can manage Google advertising preferences through My Ad Center. We do not add advertising trackers simply by publishing this policy. If we introduce website advertising measurement or remarketing technologies, we will update the relevant disclosures and provide choices where required.

5. When information is shared

We share information with service providers as needed to host and secure the website, deliver email and communications, and assist with your request. If your inquiry requires a product or installation partner, we may share the details needed to prepare the requested solution. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and security, or in connection with a business transfer subject to appropriate protections.

We do not sell inquiry contact information or provide it to third parties for their unrelated marketing.

6. Cookies, fonts, and external services

The current website contact form does not require an account or use browser storage to save your inquiry. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which involves a connection to Google and may disclose connection information such as your IP address and browser details. Hosting and security providers may use technical information necessary to deliver and protect the site. Browser settings allow you to control cookies and other website permissions.

The Customer Login link opens a separate fleet service. Vehicle locations, video, driver records, and other operational fleet data are outside the scope of this marketing website policy and are governed by the applicable service agreement and privacy notices. External websites and services have their own privacy practices.

7. Retention and security

We retain inquiry information for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain relevant business records, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage can guarantee complete security.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may ask us to stop marketing follow-up or request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting us below. Depending on your location and applicable law, additional privacy rights may apply. We will review requests under applicable requirements, may need to verify your identity, and may retain information where permitted or required by law. To appeal a privacy request decision, contact us using the same details and identify your request as an appeal.

9. Children

Our website is intended for business customers and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and address it.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our website or practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest update.
